# opencodies plan

The game work (D3, DR1, FH ports) is in `docs/README.md` under "Next". This plan holds the fork work:
parts with no game behind them.

## Workstream F: car against car

gravel-party needs cars that hit each other. No telemetry with two cars exists, so this part is
hand-rolled and not game correct. It lives in `sim/src/fork/` only. `shared/`, `d3/`, `dr1/` and `fh/`
do not change, so every bit-exact check stays valid. `sim_collide_cars` runs after each car's
`sim_step`. It takes D3 cars with a hull.

Decisions:

- The contacts and the solve must be deterministic across JS engines. Pairs go in index order
  (a < b, then piece order). No hash map iteration, no sort that is not stable.
- The solve reuses the ideas of `shared/chassis.zig` (soft PGS rows, a friction patch, a position
  pass), but each row acts on two bodies.
- The pair friction is low, so that bodies slide apart and do not lock. The value is tuned in the
  gravel-party arena.

Order: `car-pair-contact`, then `car-pair-solve`, then `car-pair-friction`.

Measure each step with tests in `sim/tests/fork.zig`, on the made-up cars:

1. Two cars head on at equal speed: the momentum stays within 1%, and the hulls separate.
2. A car pushes a car at rest from the side: no hull goes more than 5 cm into the other.
3. A car sits on the roof of another car: it does not sink through.
4. The same run gives the same state bits twice.
